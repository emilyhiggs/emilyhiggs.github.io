Emily Higgs Kopin is a GLAM professional and digital strategist with over a decade of experience in archives, digital preservation, and library systems. As Head of Digital Collections Strategy at Swarthmore College Libraries, she leads the libraries' digital asset management ecosystem, oversees policies and workflows for discoverability and preservation, and directs cross-institutional initiatives ranging from a multi-year DAMS migration to a campus digital sustainability research project. Her work sits at the intersection of technical infrastructure and cultural stewardship, covering how digital systems interrelate, what they cost to maintain, and what it actually means to build something that lasts. Emily brings a grounded, critical perspective to conversations about AI and emerging technology: interested in what these tools can genuinely do on real collections, and clear-eyed about what they can't. She is a vocal advocate for the visibility of digital infrastructure labor and for sustainable, community-owned approaches to digital collections. Emily has presented at national and regional conferences including SAA, Code4Lib, and MARAC, and her published work includes contributions to *The Routledge Companion to Libraries, Archives, and the Digital Humanities* and *The American Archivist*. She holds an MSIS from the University of Texas at Austin and a BA from Rice University.
